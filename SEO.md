# Ghana Salary Calculator — SEO Strategy

## Objective

Capture organic search traffic from people looking for salary, PAYE, SSNIT, gross-to-net and take-home-pay information in Ghana.

## Primary Search Intent

Ghana salary calculator (2026), salary calculator Ghana, take home salary Ghana, net salary calculator Ghana, gross salary to net salary Ghana, PAYE calculator Ghana, SSNIT calculator Ghana, salary breakdown Ghana.

Current-year pages must only be published when the underlying statutory values have been verified.

## Core Pages

`/salary-calculator`, `/ghana-salary-calculator`, `/take-home-pay`, `/gross-to-net`, `/paye-calculator`, `/ssnit-calculator`, `/salary-breakdown`, `/salary-guide`.

## Informational Content

Gross vs net salary; what is PAYE; what is SSNIT; how Ghana salary deductions work; how to calculate take-home pay; how to read a Ghana payslip; monthly vs annual salary; understanding salary offers.

## Current-Year Content

Pages such as `/ghana-salary-calculator-2026` only after all relevant rates and thresholds are verified for that year. Never automatically change the year in content without updating and verifying the underlying calculation configuration.

## Metadata

Every public page: unique title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph metadata, one clear H1, logical H2/H3 structure.

## Structured Data

Use schema markup where appropriate. Do not fabricate ratings, reviews, testimonials, statistics or authors.

## Internal Linking

PAYE article → PAYE calculator → salary calculator → salary breakdown guide.

## Search Trust

Show calculation assumptions, applicable year, limitations. Avoid exaggerated claims. Avoid pretending to be government/payroll software. Clearly label estimates.

## Performance

Prioritize mobile performance.

## Indexing

Index useful public pages. Do not index user-specific calculation state, private dashboards or temporary calculator state.

## Principle

The SEO page should answer the user's question even before they use the calculator.
