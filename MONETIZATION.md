# Ghana Salary Calculator — Monetization Strategy

## Primary Revenue

Advertising (potential provider: Google AdSense). Do not assume the owner has an approved account.

## Advertising Locations

Homepage, educational articles, salary guides, public calculator information pages, results page where appropriate.

## Calculator Experience

Ads must never: cover inputs, cover results, resemble calculator buttons, appear as fake calculation controls, cause accidental clicks, interrupt calculations, hide important financial information. The calculator remains free.

## Free Features

Calculate gross-to-net salary, view estimated deductions and take-home, switch monthly/annual views, read salary explanations. Do not artificially limit basic calculations.

## Future Premium Features

Salary comparison, advanced payslip calculator, salary history, employer tools, premium (downloadable) reports.

## Affiliate Revenue

Career courses, professional certifications, recruitment platforms, financial education, career services. Affiliate relationships should be transparent.

## SEO Funnel

Google search → salary information page → calculator → calculation → related salary content → returning visitor → advertising.

## Important Principle

Do not sacrifice trust for advertising revenue.
